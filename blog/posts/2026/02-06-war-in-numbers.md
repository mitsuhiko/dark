# Cold Numbers

Ukraine faces one of the harshest winters in recent memory while Russia steps up
its attacks on civilian energy infrastructure.  There was a time we would have
condemned a direct war on civilian infrastructure as a war crime. Now it
barely makes the news.

**January 2026**

* **217** strikes on energy infrastructure
* **9** waves of coordinated strikes on energy infrastructure
* **4,000** drones launched
* **350** missiles launched
* **1,000,000** households without power
* Thousands of buildings without heating
* Average daytime high of **-2°C**
* Average nighttime low of **-7°C**

2025 has been the deadliest year for civilians in Ukraine since the beginning of
the war: **2,514** dead, **12,142** injured according to HRMMU.  As of October
2025, Russia has killed **661** and injured **2,200** Ukrainian children.
